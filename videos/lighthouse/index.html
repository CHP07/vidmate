Midnight. A black sea. Marina lights the lighthouse, and for the lost boats, it's the only hope left. Every survivor on the water turns toward the beam. Dozens of boats. One light. One chance. But at 12:15, Marina sees it. In every single boat, one passenger was already infected. Red eyes reach the shore. Marina sprints up the tower, and the only way to stop them is the lamp she lit. She smashed the lamp. 12:22 AM. The sea went silent, and the island vanished into the dark.
